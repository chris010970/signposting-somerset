# Signposting Somerset

**Find local mental health and wellbeing support in Somerset, quickly.**

👉 **https://chris010970.github.io/signposting-somerset/main/**

> **Demo version.** Details are checked against each provider's own information, but services change. Please confirm with the service before attending.

---

## The problem

When someone is struggling, the right support is often already out there: a peer group, a walking group, a pain café, a carers' group, help with money or housing. Finding it is the hard part.

- **Information is scattered** across dozens of websites, PDF timetables, flyers, Facebook pages and noticeboards, each laid out differently.
- **It goes out of date quickly.** Groups move, change days or stop, and listings rarely say so.
- **The details that matter are often missing:** who it's for, whether you can just turn up, whether it costs anything, and when it actually runs.
- **National directories list organisations, not what's happening locally this week.** A search for a small Somerset town can return services miles away, or in another county entirely.

So support workers end up relying on memory, word of mouth, or a long search in the middle of a conversation with someone who needs help now.

## What Signposting Somerset does

It brings local support together in one place, checked and kept current, so a support worker can answer *"what's out there for this person, near them, that they can actually get to?"* in seconds.

- **What's on, not just who exists.** For example, the Tuesday 10am gentle walk in Wells, the 2nd-Monday carers' group, or the Hearing Voices group in Bridgwater, each with its day, time and place.
- **On a map, near the person.** Search within a few miles of any town, or any point on the map, and see what's on today or this week.
- **The practical details:** who it's for, eligibility, whether it's drop-in or needs a referral, cost, and how to get in touch.
- **Wellbeing in the broad sense.** It covers specialist support (crisis prevention, bereavement, hearing voices, long-term pain, eating disorders), practical help (money, housing, food) and everyday wellbeing (connection, activity, learning and nature, built on the Five Ways to Wellbeing).
- **Phone and online support too,** including helplines that are open right now.
- **Safety built in.** Crisis routes (999, NHS 111 option 2, Mindline, Samaritans) are on every screen. Sensitive venues, such as Crisis Safe Spaces, are never published.

Today it covers **over 300 services from around 30 local and national providers**: Mind in Somerset, Second Step, Open Mental Health, Thrive, SASP health walks, Age UK, Somerset Carers, Citizens Advice, community centres, and many smaller groups that rarely appear in any directory.

## Who it's for

Mainly **support workers, volunteers and anyone helping someone else find support**: people who need a reliable answer quickly. Anyone looking for support for themselves is welcome to use it too.

## How it's kept accurate

- **Every service is checked against its source** and dated. Records not checked for three months are flagged.
- **Nothing is guessed.** If a provider doesn't say something, it's left blank rather than filled in.
- **The records live in a shared spreadsheet,** so the people who know local services best can keep them up to date. The website shows changes within minutes.

## Feedback

Spotted something out of date, or know a service that should be here? [**Suggest a change**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf8NCl7EpGkgKNACUMDJEFzToJnoQp_xs7Fj0E9z4o2pLiqOQ/viewform). First-hand local knowledge is what keeps it useful. Please don't include personal details about anyone you support.
